Customer policies

Clear expectations for purchases, subscriptions, memberships, shipping, and support. Last approved July 17, 2026.

CBD, body-care, and cooking products

These consumable and personal-care products are final sale. Damaged and incorrect orders are handled under the exception below.

Damaged or incorrect orders

Email budlysupport@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and photos of the item and packaging. Replacement and refund requests are reviewed individually.

Physical books

Physical books may be returned within 14 days if unused and in their original condition. Customers pay return shipping unless the order was damaged or incorrect.

Digital products

Digital products and downloaded books are final sale after delivery or download.

Courses

A refund may be requested before course access is delivered. Courses are final sale after access, materials, or participation begins.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Cancel through your customer account profile before the next renewal. If profile cancellation is unavailable or unsuccessful, email budlysupport@gmail.com. Orders already processed are not automatically refundable.

Shipping

Rates and available destinations appear at checkout. Delivery dates are estimates and are not guaranteed. If an order cannot be fulfilled to the selected destination, the affected order will be refunded.

NFT memberships

Membership begins when the transaction finishes processing. You may cancel within 7 days for a full refund if you have not used the membership. Using a membership discount counts as using the membership and disqualifies the purchase from a refund. Benefits are limited to the terms displayed at purchase. Membership does not promise financial return, resale value, or appreciation.

Wholesale

Prices, minimums, shipping, labeling, and commercial terms available through the website may be completed there. Requests outside the website’s available options may require support assistance. Email budlysupport@gmail.com.

Support

We normally respond within 3 business days. Contact budlysupport@gmail.com.
