# Five sales journeys

The agent now routes discovery into one controlled journey.

1. **Consumer wellness** — compares topical, oil, bundle, and one-time versus monthly formats. Medical and dosage questions go to a person.
2. **Culinary** — compares infused cooking-oil sizes or redirects education needs to books and courses. It does not recommend a serving or dose.
3. **Books and courses** — separates reading, botanical art, culinary classes, cultivation classes, combined classes, and payment-plan interest.
4. **NFT memberships** — identifies character and tier interest while avoiding all investment, profit, appreciation, and resale claims.
5. **Wholesale** — collects business, product, quantity, destination, and timeline, then hands the opportunity to a person. It never makes a quote or commitment.

Every journey ends with one verified product page or a human handoff. A link is not represented as a completed sale, and external checkout terms must be verified before the agent states them.
